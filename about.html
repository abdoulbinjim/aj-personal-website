01 MY STORY

A LITTLE BACKGROUND

A few places.
A lot of chapters.

I've called different places home, and each has been part of my journey.

My school years took me through Lagos, Minna, and Yola. I studied Software Engineering at the American University of Nigeria, completed my national service, and began building my career in technology and financial services.

These days, I'm in Egypt, pursuing a Master's degree in Cybersecurity at the Arab Academy for Science, Technology & Maritime Transport.

Between work, school, and the ventures I'm involved in, there's usually something keeping me busy. But I also make room for the people, experiences, and interests that matter to me.
